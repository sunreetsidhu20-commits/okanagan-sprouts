Our Approach

Growing roots, building wings

At Okanagan Sprouts, every day blends free play with gentle structure. Children explore, create, and discover at their own pace, inside a daily rhythm that helps them feel safe, settled, and ready to learn. We care for infants, toddlers, and preschoolers together, so siblings and friends can grow side by side in one warm, familiar space.

Our Philosophy

Play-based. Purposefully structured.

Play-Based Learning

Children learn best with their hands, not just their ears. Blocks, art, sensory bins, dramatic play, and outdoor time give children room to follow their own curiosity and make their own discoveries every day.

A Structured Environment

A steady daily rhythm — circle time, meals, rest, and outdoor play at set times — helps children know what comes next. That predictability builds the security children need to play, explore, and grow with confidence.

Both work together: open-ended play, inside a rhythm children can count on.
